# mesa — image generation brief

The goal is consistency: every image must look like it came from the same brand.
Always prepend the **STYLE BLOCK**, then add the dish/scene.

## STYLE BLOCK (prepend to every recipe image)
Warm editorial home-cooking photograph. Natural soft window light from the left,
gentle shadows, shallow depth of field. Matte ceramic dishware in terracotta,
sage green and cream; surface is pale linen or light oak. Cozy, homey,
unstyled-but-tidy — not glossy studio stock. Muted warm palette, not
over-saturated. No text, no hands, no logos, clean uncluttered background.
Square 1:1 crop, shot from directly overhead.

## Recipe cards (swap the dish)
- …A shallow bowl of saffron rice with golden garlic chicken, scattered parsley and a lemon wedge.
- …A cast-iron pan of shakshuka — eggs in spiced tomato sauce, crumbled feta, fresh cilantro, a slice of bread on the side.
- …A wooden board with grilled salmon, charred green beans and a small dish of herb yogurt.
- …(your dish here)

## Login hero (only if going photo on the brand panel)
Use the STYLE BLOCK but replace the last line with:
"4:5 vertical crop, 45° angle, extra negative space on the left for text."
Dish: a generous family-style spread — a main, two small sides, fresh herbs and
a halved lemon — on cream linen.

## Logo mark (favicon / app icon)
Minimal single-line logo mark of a shallow bowl with one terracotta dot/steam
accent above it. Geometric, balanced, terracotta on transparent, legible at
24px and as an app icon, no text.

## Palette reference
- Terracotta (primary): #c0562a
- Sage green: #52735b
- Cream (background): #faf6f0
- Warm near-black (text): #1a1612
